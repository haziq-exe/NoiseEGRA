# Where the research stands

Read this before running anything. `AGENTS.md` covers the mechanics (Kaggle,
tests, git rules); this covers what the project is trying to show, which
experiments have been run and what they returned, and the measurement faults that
have produced wrong numbers here.

It reports results, not conclusions. Most numbers here come from a single run on
a single model at a single layer band, and the configurations differ between
experiments — two models, two layer bands, three requirement sets, samples from 24
to 100 stories. Read the setup line above each table before carrying a result
anywhere.

Companion documents:

- `METHODS_TRIED.md` — every condition ever run, what it did, what it scored.
  Generated by `scripts/build_method_registry.py`. Worth checking before building
  a mechanism: a suite was once written and nearly launched for something that had
  been run thirteen rounds earlier, on a different model.
- `EXPERIMENT_LOG.md` — the narrative, round by round: what was asked, what came
  back, what it changed.

## The goal, stated precisely

A cheap inference-time method that **at the same time**

1. improves constraint compliance over the unmodified model, and
2. improves output diversity over the unmodified model,

and beats what you get for free by turning the sampling temperature up. Either
one alone is easy and neither counts on its own. The method must be an
extension of the original architecture — **constraint steering plus noise** —
not a replacement for it.

Diversity is the Vendi score: the effective number of distinct stories in a set.
Compliance is the mean number of requirements broken per story, out of fifteen.

## Where the headline comparison stands (r17, 2026-09-16)

*r17-headline. Qwen3-1.7B, layers 10-18, the fifteen one-sided requirements all
asked for in the prompt, 100 stories per condition, Vendi on the first 40 words,
the decoding grid and the method's frontier in one run. One run, one model.*

Raw, the method looks like the answer: push 3 + perturbation 0.15 scores 4.94
broken and Vendi 9.73 against the default's 8.43 and 6.64, and push 4.5 +
perturbation 0.25 (5.01, 12.74) beats every decoding arm on both axes. **The raw
winners are broken text.** The coherence checks added the same day flag 100 of
100 stories in every push-4.5 arm ("Mia runs. Mia! Mia. Mia. Lily. Lily."), 48-58
of 100 in the push-3 arms, and 19-32 of 100 in the decoding arms. Scored only
over stories that pass the checks, at matched group size (full tables in
`EXPERIMENT_LOG.md`, round 17):

- the compliance win is real: push-3 arms break 4.3-5.5 of fifteen among clean
  stories against 7.4-7.9 for every decoding arm;
- the clean structural-diversity win is not: temperature 1.8 reaches 21.5-23.3
  effective structurally-distinct stories, the best push arm 21.6, the headline
  arm 16.8 (baseline 16.4). The method's raw Vendi advantage was degenerate
  outliers plus phrasing variety;
- the perturbation alone lifts structural diversity to 20-24 at a decoding-arm
  keep rate; the push alone drops it to 13.9 and halves the keep rate;
- the method still beats the *unmodified default* on both axes however scored,
  but it keeps 42-51 coherent stories per 100 where the temperatures keep 73-81.

So against tuned decoding the method wins compliance by three requirements and
loses clean structural diversity: neither dominates. Every push arm ran at
temperature 1.0, the regime where the model loops unaided; the method at
temperature 1.6-1.8 — each half fixing the other's failure mode — has never been
run and is the open cell.

**Caveat that reframes the mechanism, not the trade.** Every perturbation arm in
r17-headline ran with the isotropic-fallback bug above, so what the table calls
the "story-difference basis" was isotropic noise. The compliance column is
unaffected (the push drives it), and the diversity conclusions stand for
*isotropic* perturbation. Whether the sampled basis does better is answered by
r17-controls, next.

## The sampled basis does not beat isotropic noise (r17-controls, 2026-09-16)

*Nine conditions x 100 stories, same model/prompt/requirements/truncation as
r17-headline, and the first run to actually build the story-difference basis.
Diversity from `scripts/score_structure.py` over coherent stories, rarefied to 40
per condition, 40 draws; "structural" = Vendi over each story's content-lemma set
(names excluded), "syntactic" = Vendi over its part-of-speech trigram profile.*

The perturbation at gamma 0.15, three ways, alone and under the push at 3:

| arm | kept/100 | broken/15 | structural | syntactic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| story-difference basis, alone | 70 | 8.25 | 27.70 ±0.63 | 12.62 |
| isotropic draw, alone | 75 | 8.22 | 25.93 ±0.87 | 14.08 |
| projection removed, alone | 78 | 8.57 | 27.07 ±0.93 | 14.00 |
| story-difference basis + push 3 | 52 | 4.84 | 20.41 ±0.73 | 9.92 |
| isotropic draw + push 3 | 48 | 4.94 | 20.34 ±0.40 | 8.90 |
| projection removed + push 3 | 48 | 5.32 | 22.37 ±0.62 | 9.76 |

Two findings, both against the method's story so far.

**The sampled basis buys essentially nothing over an isotropic draw.** Alone it
is 1.8 structural-diversity points ahead (27.70 against 25.93) but keeps fewer
coherent stories (70 against 75) and is *lower* on syntactic diversity; under the
push the two are identical (20.41 against 20.34). The between-generation basis is
the object the novelty claim rests on ([[egra-novelty-tension]]), and on the
first run that actually uses it, a random direction of the same length does as
well. The earlier claim that the basis performs better (Vendi 8.57 against 6.53)
came from a Qwen3-8B run whose basis construction was not verified and may carry
the same fallback.

**The projection buys compliance, not diversity.** Removing it (the "free" arm)
leaves diversity equal or higher — 22.37 against 20.41 structural under the push —
while compliance falls, 4.84 to 5.32 broken. That is the cleanest read yet of
what the projection is for: it protects the requirements the offset would
otherwise disturb (r2 found the same on Qwen3-8B, less cleanly), and it is not
paying for that protection in diversity. So of the two ideas that make the
perturbation more than plain noise, one (the projection) earns its ~0.5
requirement and the other (the sampled basis) does not earn anything measurable.

## The method

Two mechanisms applied to the residual stream at layers 10–18.

**The constraint push** supplies compliance. One direction per requirement,
extracted by contrastive mean difference from twelve length-matched minimal pairs
each (`noiseegra/data/steering_pairs_en.json`), made mutually orthogonal by
Löwdin, summed, and renormalised so the *total* push has a fixed length. The
renormalisation matters: without it, summing k directions at coefficient beta
gives a push of length beta·sqrt(k), so adding a requirement silently raises the
dose, and the number of requirements and the strength of the intervention are the
same knob.

**The perturbation** supplies diversity. One constant offset per story, drawn
from the subspace along which whole stories' activations differ from one another,
projected clear of the constraint directions so it cannot move a requirement
either way, and applied to the prompt positions only — the model is moved
somewhere else before it writes a token and then decodes unperturbed, so a large
shift costs no fluency.

Both are one vector added per layer. Inference cost is the same as generation.

## Experiments run, and what they returned

Each entry gives the setup before the numbers. **The setup differs between
entries in ways that can carry the result**: two models, two layer bands, three
requirement sets, and sample sizes from 24 to 100. A result measured on one
configuration has not been shown to hold on another. Where an entry is the only
measurement of its kind, that is said.

The configurations that appear:

| rounds | model | layers | requirement set | stories/condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| qwen-generic, qwen-round2-5 | **Qwen3-8B** | 14-22 | 12 mixed | 24-100 |
| r7-r9 | Qwen3-1.7B | 10-18 | 12-13 mixed | 24-40 |
| r10-r14 | Qwen3-1.7B | 10-18 | 13 mixed | 40 |
| r15-r16 | Qwen3-1.7B | 10-18 | 13 one-sided | 30-100 |
| r17 (running) | Qwen3-1.7B | 10-18 | 15 one-sided | 100 |

"Mixed" sets contain requirements of the form "between N and M"; "one-sided" sets
do not. All runs use the generic task: one instruction, no scenario, every story
generated from the same prompt, temperature 1.0 and the checkpoint's own cut-offs
unless stated. `METHODS_TRIED.md` lists every condition; `EXPERIMENT_LOG.md` has
the round each came from.

### Requirements of the form "between N and M", under a constant push

*Rounds 7-14. Qwen3-1.7B, layers 10-18, the mixed sets, 24-40 stories per
condition, 36 steered conditions in total. Four such requirements in the set:
word count 50-65, sentence count 6-8, every sentence 4-10 words, exactly two
quoted lines.*

Pass rates on that family: 23% unsteered, 3% under a constant push at a total
budget of 2, 9-14% when the push was scaled by the constraint error measured on
the partial decoded text. Two of the 36 conditions came in above the unsteered
rate, both at 24% against 23%.

On the same runs the one-sided requirements in the same set went from 25%
unsteered to 64-66% under the same pushes.

Not tested on Qwen3-8B, and not tested with any mechanism other than a constant
coefficient, an activation-space feedback term, and error-scaled coefficients.

### Steering and perturbation, each alone and together

*r16. Qwen3-1.7B, layers 10-18, the 13 one-sided requirements, eight directions
(plain_words excluded), 100 stories per condition, diversity on the first 40 words
of each story, embedding model Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B.*

| condition | broken/13 | Vendi |
|---|---|---|
| the model as it ships | 7.78 | 6.93 |
| constraint push at 3, alone | 3.94 | 5.67 |
| perturbation at 0.1, alone | 7.08 | 9.94 |
| push at 3 + perturbation at 0.1 | 3.72 | 8.26 |
| push at 3 + perturbation at 0.15 | 3.84 | 9.56 |

The perturbation here is a per-story constant offset drawn from the story-difference
subspace, projected clear of the constraint directions, applied at the prompt
positions only.

This is one run on one model at one layer band. Not repeated, and not run on
Qwen3-8B.

### The size of the push

| total push | broken/13 | looping | words |
|---|---|---|---|

### Perturbing the constraint vector itself, at unchanged length

*Round 2. **Qwen3-8B**, layers 14-22, 12 mixed requirements, five directions, 40
stories per condition, no steering budget in force at the time (coefficients were
summed unnormalised). Baseline 4.55 broken and Vendi 4.03, untruncated.*

Turning the summed constraint vector by atan(kappa) with its norm preserved, kappa
1: 5.03 broken, Vendi 4.15. Drawing a per-requirement gain per story, spread 0.5:
5.15 broken, Vendi 3.60. Adding a perpendicular step, which does increase the total
length: kappa 0.1 gave 4.97 broken and Vendi 5.14; kappa 0.15 gave 5.95 broken and
Vendi 6.59.

*r15 repeated only the re-weighting, on Qwen3-1.7B at budget 3 with the 13
one-sided requirements and 100 stories:* 5.33 broken against 4.71 for the same push
without it, and looping 33% against 24%.

Turning the vector has **not** been retried on Qwen3-1.7B, on the one-sided
requirement set, or under a steering budget. Its only measurement is on a different
model, a different layer band, a different requirement set and an unnormalised
push.

### Choosing the set of per-story perturbations rather than drawing each one

*r16. Qwen3-1.7B, layers 10-18, the 13 one-sided requirements, 100 stories per
condition, offsets drawn from a rank-72 story-difference basis after the constraint
subspace was removed.*

The whole set laid out in advance by repulsion so the offsets spread over the
subspace. Same length per offset, same subspace, mean pairwise similarity 0.29
either way, worst pair 0.90 down to 0.67. Four matched pairs against independent
draws:

| pair | drawn independently | set chosen together |
|---|---|---|
| perturbation 0.1 alone | 9.94 | 9.68 |
| perturbation 0.15 alone | 12.71 | 11.94 |
| push 3 + perturbation 0.1 | 8.26 | 7.95 |
| push 3 + perturbation 0.15 | 9.56 | 8.81 |

Implemented as `offset_draw="spread"`, default off. One run, one model; the

### What collapse does to the requirement set

Thirteen of the fifteen requirements get easier as writing falls apart: short
sentences, short words, a plain first sentence, no subordinate clauses. "The Rabbit
jumps. The Rabbit sleeps. The Rabbit Bites. The Rabbit Bites. The Rabbit Bites."
passes nine of them, and the repeated-five-word-run rule misses it because the
repeating unit is three words.

Two requirements were added in response — no sentence written twice, no two words
beginning more than three sentences — and `tests/test_english_smoke.py` asserts a
deliberately collapsed story fails them.

*r16 re-scored on all fifteen. The prompt used in r16 asked for thirteen, so the
two added ones were not requested of the model in these stories; this is a
diagnostic, not a headline table. r17 is the first run whose prompt asks for all
fifteen.*

| condition | broken/15 | no duplicate sentence | fresh openings |
|---|---|---|---|
| the model as it ships | 8.60 | 71% | 47% |
| push 3 alone | 5.19 | 28% | 47% |
| perturbation 0.1 alone | 7.67 | 74% | 67% |
| push 3 + perturbation 0.1 | 4.61 | 38% | 73% |

Counting a story as degenerate if a three-gram repeats above 25%, a five-gram above
15%, or an opening is used four or more times: 64% of the unmodified model's
stories, 54% under the push alone, 41% under the perturbation alone, 33% under
both.

## Measurement faults found here, and what they did

These are recorded because each one produced a number that was reported and then
turned out to be wrong.

**Degenerate text scores well on both metrics.** A set of differently-broken
stories is mutually dissimilar, so looping raises Vendi, and collapsed text
trivially satisfies requirements like "at most two adverbs". A result was reported
whose best arm contained *"I'm going to the store"* eight times. Repetition is now
among the scored requirements and every table prints a degeneracy column, but
neither catches everything — reading the stories is what caught the collapse
described above (`scripts/read_stories.py`, or index into the state file).

**The n-gram looping rules undercount degeneration, and they undercount it most
in the steered arms.** Read on 2026-09-16: stories that pass the repeated-n-gram
rules and every coherence heuristic of the time include loops whose unit varies
each pass (*'The leaves sang," Tommy said.'* with rotating names — the vocabulary
is tiny but no five-gram repeats), stalls that change one word per repeat (*"She
felt happy. She was happy. She felt good."*), collapse into fragments too short
to hold an n-gram (*"Mia. Balloon. Fly."*), and broken dialogue with quote marks
opening and closing at random. Four checks now catch these (`noiseegra/coherence.py`:
windowed vocabulary entropy, near-duplicate-sentence ratio, tiny-sentence runs,
quote density), thresholds calibrated on hand-read stories from this project's
own runs — zero fires on the two clean conditions read (the sensory-direction
probe and temperature 1.8), and every flagged story that was read in the baseline
and method arms was genuinely broken. On r16's saved stories the full checker
flags **46-49% of the best arm's stories** (push 3 + perturbation) against the
15-19% the looping column reported, and 41% of the baseline's against 32%. Any
diversity number over unfiltered stories inherits that gap; the r16 headline
Vendi has not been re-scored under the filter, and r17 should be read with
`scripts/score_structure.py` (locally, no GPU) alongside the kernel's Vendi.

**One embedding Vendi cannot say what kind of variety it is counting.**
`noiseegra/structure.py` splits it: structural diversity (each story reduced to
its set of content lemmas, names excluded — what happens in it) and syntactic
diversity (part-of-speech trigram profiles — how its sentences are shaped).
Diagnostic on r16's coherent stories at 40-word truncation, rarefied to 51 per
condition (prompt asked for thirteen requirements, so this is not a headline
table): the perturbation alone raises structural diversity from the baseline's
22.6 to 28-32; the push alone lowers it to 19.2; push plus perturbation lands at
21.6-23.6, near the baseline. Syntactic diversity nearly doubles under the
method (5.1 → 9.2-9.9). So on that round the method's measured Vendi gain over
baseline is carried more by phrasing variety and by degenerate outliers than by
different things happening — the question r17 should answer on the
fifteen-requirement prompt. `vendi_from_embeddings` also now takes a Rényi
order `q` (default 1, the published form): quoting q=1 beside q=2 says whether
a gain lives in the bulk of the stories or in a few outliers, since broken
stories enter q=1 at full weight and q=2 barely.

**Vendi rises with length.** Steering shortens the stories — 47 words against the
baseline's 85 — so an untruncated comparison partly measures that. Always pass
`--truncate-words 40`, and never compare a Vendi measured under one truncation
with one measured under another.

**Qwen3 ships top_p and top_k in its generation config.** A condition that does
not set them inherits them, so the "plain" baseline is not untruncated sampling.
A top-p 0.95 arm at temperature 1.0 once came back identical to the baseline in
every digit across a hundred stories.

**The story-difference basis was never built for r16-spread or r17-headline, so
their "story-basis" arms were isotropic noise.** (Found 2026-09-16.) The runner
builds the sampled activation basis only when the requested suite is in a
hardcoded guard set, and that set had drifted: `spread`, `frontier` and `select`
were all missing from it. When the guard did not fire, `args.offset_basis` stayed
`None`, and a per-story offset with no basis falls back to an isotropic draw
projected clear of the constraints — while the run id still reads `obstory`. So
every arm labelled "per-story offset drawn from the story-difference subspace" in
r16-spread and r17-headline was in fact an isotropic perturbation. Proof: the
r17-controls run (which does build the basis) reproduces r17-headline's
"story-basis" arm byte-for-byte from its *isotropic* arm, 100 stories of 100,
while its real story-basis arm differs. Fixed: `BASIS_SUITES` is now module-level
with the three suites added, the runner crashes if any `obstory`/`obprompt` run id
survives with no basis, and `tests/test_suites_build.py` builds every suite with
no basis and asserts any that names one is guarded. What this costs: the r16
"best result" and every r17-headline diversity number attributed to the story
basis is really a measurement of isotropic noise. That is not fatal, because
r17-controls shows the two are nearly the same (below), but the labels were wrong.

**Run ids must record every setting that differs.** Two arms whose ids collide
share a file and the second silently overwrites the first — a wrong answer with
no error. `tests/test_suites_build.py` builds every suite in the runner's
`--suite` choices and asserts distinct ids; it takes its list from the runner, so
a new suite cannot escape it.

**Sharded runs write one score file per GPU.** The merge used to let one
overwrite the other, so half the diversity numbers vanished and a missing row
looks exactly like a condition that was never run. Fixed; `scripts/read_monotone.py`
also recovers them from the live log.

**Numbers are comparable only within a round.** Each round used its own
requirement set, prompt and sometimes its own model. Never build a table across
rounds.

## Reading a run

    python scripts/read_monotone.py experiments/<name> --name <name> [--full]

Prints compliance, diversity, looping and length per condition, sorted, against
the unmodified model, and marks arms that beat it on both axes. Condition names
are written out as descriptions, not assembled from setting names.

## In flight

`r17-headline` — landed 2026-09-16; results above and in `EXPERIMENT_LOG.md`
round 17.

`r17-controls` (on the second account, haziqaus) — nine conditions x 100 stories
answering which parts of the per-story perturbation earn their place, same model,
prompt, fifteen requirements and 40-word truncation as `r17-headline`, with its
own baseline and method arms inside the run so every comparison is internal.
Three surgeries at gamma 0.15, each with and without the push at 3: the
constraint-span projection removed (`offset_mode free`); the story-difference
basis replaced by an isotropic draw of the same length (the control for whether
the sampled basis, and not the mere fact of a per-story shift, carries the
diversity); and, with the push only, the offset kept on while the model writes
(retrying the injection-site result now that the budget and the one-sided set
changed what steering does).

## Open questions

- r17 answered "does the method beat the temperature curve" with a trade:
  compliance yes by three requirements, clean structural diversity no, and the
  push at temperature 1.0 degenerates half its stories. The open cell is the
  method at temperature 1.6-1.8, where the model does not loop unaided —
  untested, and the natural r18.
- The push at 4.5 collapses every story at temperature 1.0 on the fifteen-
  requirement prompt. Whether a higher temperature revives it is part of the
  same cell.
- The perturbation is applied at the prompt only. Applying it while writing was
  worse in earlier rounds, but has not been retried since the budget and the
  monotone set changed what steering does.
- Three mechanisms are implemented and have never run on a GPU: perturbation
  *not* projected clear of the constraints (the ablation that would show the
  projection earns its place), random directions instead of extracted ones (the
  control that would show the directions are not just noise), and activation-space
  feedback.
- Whether any representation-level intervention can improve a requirement of the
  form "between N and M". 36 steered conditions on Qwen3-1.7B did not; no other
  model has been tried, and the mechanisms tried were constant coefficients,
  activation-space feedback, and error-scaled coefficients.
- Whether any of the above holds on a second model. Everything from r7 onward is
  Qwen3-1.7B at layers 10-18. The earlier Qwen3-8B rounds used a different layer
  band and a different requirement set, so they are not a replication of anything
  measured since.
- Turning the constraint vector at constant length was measured once, on Qwen3-8B,
  with an unnormalised push and the mixed requirement set. Whether it behaves the
  same on Qwen3-1.7B under a budget is untested.
